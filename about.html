What is Floristry & Garden Designing

Floristry and garden design are elegant arts that transform spaces through the beauty of flowers and the harmony of nature.

  • Aesthetics: visual harmony
  • Composition: arrangement
  • Texture: depth and interest
  • Seasonality: reflect or thriv
  • Functionality: designing for use
  • Sustainability: eco-friendly
  • Color Theory : color combinations
  • Personal Expression: individual style

In both floristry and garden design, art meets nature to create spaces that inspire, comfort, and delight. Through careful attention to color, texture, seasonality, and personal expression, these practices bring beauty and purpose to everyday environments. Together, they reflect a timeless appreciation for the natural world shaped by human creativity.
